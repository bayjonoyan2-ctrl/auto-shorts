# 🎬 auto-shorts

![demo](assets/demo.gif)

> **Generate 9:16 YouTube Shorts from long videos for free, entirely in the cloud.**

This tool uses **GitHub Actions** as a free server. You paste a video URL, and GitHub's cloud downloads it, crops it to vertical format, and gives you the file.

- **Zero PC resources used.**
- **100% Free.**
- **Custom Layouts:** Auto-detect facecam and game screen for streamers, or standard crop for regular videos.

## How to use

1. Open the Web UI (GitHub Pages link).
2. **Get a Token:** Go to [GitHub Settings > Tokens (classic)](https://github.com/settings/tokens), click "Generate new token (classic)", and check the **`workflow`** box. Copy the token.
3. Paste your Video URL, select the layout, and choose how many Shorts you want.
4. Click **Generate Shorts** and paste your Token when asked.
5. Click the link that appears to watch the cloud process and download your videos!
